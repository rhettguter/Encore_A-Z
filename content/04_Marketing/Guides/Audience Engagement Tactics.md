# Audience Engagement Tactics

*Five field-tested tactics for keeping Encore audiences engaged, excited, and returning. Companion to [[Marketing]].*

Engaging your audience effectively is more crucial than ever for the ongoing success of Encore productions. These five tactics help build stronger connections with the community while ensuring productions remain well-attended and financially sustainable.

## Tactic 1 — Take an Event-Driven Approach

Transform key announcements and season unveilings into memorable events that generate excitement and anticipation.

**Implementation tips**

- Make your season announcement an event itself rather than just a simple announcement.
- Consider both in-person and livestream options to reach a wider audience.
- Bring the same energy and production value to these events as you do to your shows.
- Create a memorable experience that gives your audience a taste of what's to come.
- Share behind-the-scenes insights that make attendees feel like insiders.

**Action steps**

1. Schedule a season unveiling event at your theater.
2. Include performances, special guests, and visual presentations.
3. Create promotional materials specific to the event.
4. Invite media and community leaders.
5. Capture video and photos to share with those who couldn't attend.

## Tactic 2 — Activate Your Email Marketing

Email remains one of the most effective channels for connecting with audiences and driving ticket sales.

**Implementation tips**

- Develop a consistent email schedule (monthly newsletter plus show-specific campaigns).
- Focus on clear, engaging content with compelling visuals.
- Keep communications authentic and personal.
- Include clear calls-to-action for ticket purchases.
- Highlight upcoming productions with enticing descriptions.

**Action steps**

1. Build and maintain an email list from ticket buyers and website visitors.
2. Create email templates that reflect Encore's branding.
3. Develop a content calendar for regular communications.
4. Test different subject lines and content formats to see what drives engagement.
5. Analyze open rates and click-throughs to refine your approach.

## Tactic 3 — Make Specific Donation Requests

Fundraising is more effective when you make concrete, specific requests rather than general appeals.

**Implementation tips**

- Frame donation requests around specific needs rather than general support.
- Create targeted campaigns for particular expenses (e.g., $10 to license a song).
- Make the impact of each donation clear and tangible.
- Communicate how donations directly enhance the audience experience.
- Don't be apologetic about asking for support.

**Action steps**

1. Identify specific funding needs for each production.
2. Create campaign materials that clearly communicate these needs.
3. Set up targeted donation options on your website.
4. Recognize donors in programs and other materials.
5. Share the results of successful campaigns to build trust.

## Tactic 4 — Offer Discounts, Tiered Pricing, and Targeted Showings

Increase accessibility and reach new audiences through creative pricing and programming strategies.

**Implementation tips**

- Create "pay what you can" performances for specific shows.
- Offer tiered pricing options to accommodate different budgets.
- Consider special showings for specific communities (seniors, students, etc.).
- Partner with neighborhood organizations to offer location-based discounts.
- Use targeted pricing as audience development rather than just as discounting.

**Action steps**

1. Analyze current audience demographics to identify gaps.
2. Develop pricing strategies that address financial barriers.
3. Schedule special performances aimed at specific communities.
4. Promote these initiatives through targeted channels.
5. Collect feedback to refine your approach.

## Tactic 5 — Build Encore's Brand

Establish a consistent, recognizable identity that builds trust and recognition in the community.

**Implementation tips**

- Ensure all patron interactions reflect Encore's brand values.
- Maintain consistent visual identity across all materials.
- Define what makes Encore unique in the community.
- Build relationships with local businesses and organizations.
- Create merchandise that extends brand recognition.

**Action steps**

1. Develop a clear brand statement and visual guidelines.
2. Train staff and volunteers to embody Encore's brand values.
3. Audit all customer touchpoints for brand consistency.
4. Identify partnership opportunities that align with the brand.
5. Create branded merchandise that audiences will proudly use.

## Implementation Timeline

### Short-term actions (next 30 days)

- Audit the current email list and send a re-engagement campaign.
- Identify three specific donation needs for the next production.
- Plan one special-price performance for the upcoming show.
- Review brand consistency across digital channels.

### Medium-term actions (2–3 months)

- Plan the next season announcement event.
- Develop a comprehensive email marketing calendar.
- Create a tiered pricing structure for future shows.
- Begin building community partnerships.

### Long-term actions (6+ months)

- Evaluate the success of initiatives through audience surveys.
- Refine approaches based on data and feedback.
- Expand successful programs to reach wider audiences.
- Build a sustainable system for implementing these tactics consistently.

## Measuring Success

Track these key metrics to ensure tactics are effective:

- Ticket sales (overall and by performance type)
- Email engagement rates
- Donation amounts (general vs. specific campaigns)
- New vs. returning audience members
- Social media engagement and follower growth
- Community partnerships established
- Audience survey feedback

Building audience engagement is an ongoing process. By consistently implementing these tactics while measuring results and refining your approach, Encore can build stronger connections with the community and sustain success for years to come.
