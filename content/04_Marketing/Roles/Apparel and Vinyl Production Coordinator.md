# Apparel and Vinyl Production Coordinator

## Position Overview

The Apparel & Vinyl Production Coordinator executes custom apparel production for Encore — turning approved designs into finished products (typically show t-shirts), managing inventory, and ensuring timely delivery for performances, camps, and events.

**Reports to:** [[01_Production/Roles/Production Team/Production Manager]]
**Collaborates closely with:** Graphic Designer, [[Marketing]] team, [[Box Office Manager]] (merch sales)

## Key Responsibilities

### Production Execution

- Receive finalized designs from the Graphic Designer and prepare them for vinyl cutting and application.
- Cut, weed, and apply vinyl designs to t-shirts and other apparel.
- Ensure consistency and quality across all finished products.
- Troubleshoot production issues and adjust processes as needed.

### Inventory Management

- Track and maintain inventory of blank apparel (sizes, styles, quantities).
- Monitor and manage vinyl stock, tools, and supplies.
- Anticipate needs and place orders for t-shirts and materials in a timely manner.
- Maintain organized storage of apparel and materials.

### Production Tracking

- Track quantities of items produced for each show, camp, or event.
- Maintain accurate records of orders, inventory usage, and completed items.
- Coordinate with leadership on production numbers and needs.

### Design and Timeline Coordination

- Communicate proactively with the Graphic Designer to ensure designs are received on schedule.
- Stay ahead of production timelines to meet deadlines for performances, camps, and events.
- Flag delays or issues early and collaborate on solutions.

### Collaboration and Support

- Work closely with Production and Administrative teams to determine apparel needs.
- Assist in distribution planning and fulfillment of apparel orders.
- Support additional merchandise or branding needs as assigned.

## Qualifications

### Essential

- Experience with vinyl cutting machines (Cricut, Silhouette, or similar).
- Strong attention to detail and quality control.
- Organizational and inventory management skills.
- Ability to manage time effectively and meet deadlines.

### Preferred

- Basic understanding of design file formats (SVG, PNG, etc.).
- Prior experience with merchandise or branded apparel production.

### Personal Qualities

- **Detail-oriented and precise.**
- **Organized and proactive.**
- **Reliable and deadline-driven.**
- **Problem-solver with a hands-on mindset.**

## Working Conditions

- **Commitment:** Per production, camp, or event cycle.
- **Schedule:** Heavier leading into productions, camps, and events; lighter between.
- **Environment:** Apparel production workspace; coordination with the production and marketing teams.
- **Reports to:** [[01_Production/Roles/Production Team/Production Manager]].
- **Compensation:** Per Encore's contractor structure.
