# Creative Team — README

*People making creative decisions about what the show looks and sounds like. The artistic core.*

## What lives here

Director, Director/Choreographer (combined role), Choreographer, Music Supervisor/Director. Each role typically has a paired Checklist note. **Associates/** subfolder holds apprentice-track roles (Acting/Vocal/Choreographer Associate Directors, Associate Choreographer).

## What doesn't live here

Practitioners who execute the design (set/costume/light/sound) live in `../Design Team/`. Logistics roles (PM, SM) live in `../Production Team/`.
