# Production Assistant

> [!info] At a Glance
> - **You support the production team and learn the craft.** Entry-level young-artist role.
> - **Every contribution matters.** Supporting others' work is as valuable as being in the spotlight.
> - **Your closest partners:** [[Stage Manager]], [[Assistant Stage Manager]], the creative team.
> - **Pathway:** **Production Assistant** → [[Assistant Stage Manager]] → [[Stage Manager]]. See [[Production Hierarchy]].
> - **Where you live in the process:** rehearsals through closing.

## Position Overview

The Production Assistant (PA) provides essential support to the production team while learning how a theatrical production actually comes together. This entry-level position is designed for young artists who want to explore behind-the-scenes theater work and develop foundational production skills.

The PA introduces young artists to the collaborative nature of theater — teaching that every contribution matters, and that supporting others' work is as valuable as being in the spotlight. See [[Production Hierarchy]] for how the SM/ASM/PA pathway works.

## Key Responsibilities

### General Production Support

- Assist the creative team with daily needs and tasks.
- Help with rehearsal setup and cleanup.
- Support props and costume organization.
- Assist with administrative tasks as needed.
- Run errands for production team members.

### Rehearsal Support

- Help distribute scripts, music, and rehearsal materials.
- Assist with moving furniture and set pieces.
- Support younger cast members during breaks.
- Help maintain a clean and organized rehearsal space.
- Assist with crowd management during large group scenes.

### Technical Support

- Help with basic technical setup and cleanup.
- Assist with prop placement and organization.
- Support costume and makeup organization.
- Help with backstage preparation during tech rehearsals.
- Assist with strike and cleanup after performances.

### Communication Support

- Help relay messages between departments.
- Assist with basic data entry and filing.
- Support the creation of production materials.
- Help with sign-in procedures and attendance tracking.

## Qualifications

### Essential

- Eagerness to learn and help others.
- Willingness to take on various tasks.
- Basic organizational skills.
- Ability to follow directions.
- Positive attitude and team spirit.

### Preferred

- Prior experience in an Encore production (as a performer or in another role).
- Familiarity with the rehearsal-room environment.

### Personal Qualities

- **Reliability** — shows up, on time, ready to help.
- **Curiosity** — interested in exploring theater beyond performing.
- **Positive Attitude** — brings energy that lifts the room.
- **Initiative** — sees what needs doing and does it without being asked.

## Working Conditions

- **Commitment:** Per production. Rehearsal nights and tech week; performance attendance per the [[Stage Manager]].
- **Schedule:** Evenings and weekends for rehearsals; intensive during tech week.
- **Environment:** Rehearsal rooms, backstage, theater venue.
- **Reports to:** [[Stage Manager]] (primary) and [[Assistant Stage Manager]] (day-to-day).
- **Compensation:** Volunteer / young-artist entry role. May earn class tuition credit per Encore policy.

## Educational Outcomes

Through this role, young artists develop:

- Basic understanding of theater production processes.
- Teamwork and collaboration skills.
- Reliability and work ethic.
- Communication and interpersonal skills.
- Problem-solving and adaptability.
- Appreciation for all aspects of theater creation.

For more on the young-artist mentorship philosophy, see [[Rehearsal Culture]] and the Encore Young Artist Mentorship Program section there.

## Growth Pathway

The Production Assistant role is the entry step in Encore's stage management pathway. Young artists who excel here can advance to [[Assistant Stage Manager]] in a future production, and eventually to [[Stage Manager]].
