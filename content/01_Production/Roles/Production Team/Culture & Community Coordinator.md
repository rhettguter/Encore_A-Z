# Culture & Community Coordinator

## Position Overview

The Culture & Community Coordinator cultivates, protects, and enhances the positive, inclusive, and uplifting culture of Encore Performing Arts. The role focuses on intentionally creating meaningful experiences for staff, artists, families, and patrons — ensuring people feel seen, valued, and connected throughout their time at Encore.

**Reports to:** [[Production Manager]]
**Collaborates closely with:** [[Director]], [[Stage Manager]], [[Volunteer Coordinator]], [[Event Coordinator]]

## Key Responsibilities

### Culture Building and Experience Design

- Proactively develop and implement initiatives that foster a positive, welcoming, mission-driven environment.
- Identify opportunities to create meaningful, memorable experiences for cast members, staff, and families.
- Support Encore leadership in maintaining a strong, values-centered culture across all programs.

### Recognition and Celebrations

- Track and celebrate birthdays, milestones, and achievements of staff and participants.
- Identify and highlight "magical moments" that occur during rehearsals, performances, and events.
- Coordinate recognition efforts that reinforce appreciation and belonging.

### Care and Support

- Respond with care and coordination during sensitive moments (illness, loss, family hardship).
- Help organize appropriate gestures of support (cards, messages, meals).
- Serve as a thoughtful connector so individuals feel supported during difficult times.

### Production Keepsakes and Closing Traditions

- Lead the creation of small posters and materials for cast photo books or closing-night gifts.
- Coordinate collection of photos, messages, and memories from each production.
- Help execute meaningful closing-night traditions that celebrate the experience.

### Community Engagement

- Build positive relationships with families, patrons, and participants.
- Support efforts that strengthen connection and communication within the Encore community.
- Help create a welcoming, inclusive environment at events and performances.

## Qualifications

### Essential

- Strong interpersonal and relational skills.
- High emotional intelligence and empathy.
- Excellent organization and follow-through.
- Ability to handle sensitive situations with discretion and care.
- Creative thinker with a passion for building meaningful experiences.

### Preferred

- Experience in event planning, team culture, or community engagement.
- Familiarity with Encore's culture and traditions.

### Personal Qualities

- **Thoughtful and observant.**
- **Encouraging and people-centered.**
- **Proactive and detail-oriented.**
- **Positive, uplifting presence.**

## Working Conditions

- **Commitment:** Consistent engagement throughout productions, camps, and events.
- **Schedule:** Available for key moments such as opening/closing nights and special events.
- **Environment:** Rehearsal rooms, performance venues, community events.
- **Reports to:** [[Production Manager]].
- **Compensation:** Per Encore's contractor structure.

## By Phase

### Inception

*Welcome to the Encore fam! Be sure to complete your onboarding items below — we look forward to building an incredible show that builds life skills through stage skills for young artists.*

**Onboarding** *(one-time when joining Encore)*
- Complete child safety training.
- Submit background check.
- Submit W-9.
- Sign and return contract.
- Complete onboarding process.

**Initial Production Work**

- Confirm show, dates, and key milestones with the [[Production Manager]].
- Begin planning culture moments aligned with the production calendar.

See [[1 - Inception]] for the phase overview.

### Pre-Production

- Coordinate with the [[Director]] and [[Production Manager]] on the kinds of culture moments that will fit this show.
- Plan opening-rehearsal welcome touches.

See [[2 - Pre-Production]] for the phase overview.

### Casting

- Help shape the first-rehearsal welcome experience for the new cast.
- Begin tracking birthdays, milestones, and personal details as cast information becomes available.

See [[3 - Casting]] for the phase overview.

### Rehearsals

- Attend selected rehearsals to spot magical moments and capture them.
- Coordinate small recognitions for milestones (first day off-book, halfway-through-rehearsals, etc.).
- Support the [[Director]] and [[Stage Manager]] in maintaining positive room culture.

See [[4 - Rehearsals]] for the phase overview.

### Pre-Tech

- Plan opening-night gifts, cards, or rituals.
- Coordinate any special-occasion touches for the transition into the venue.

See [[5 - Pre-Tech]] for the phase overview.

### Technical Rehearsals

- Be present for moments where the team needs extra support during long tech days.
- Coordinate food, snacks, or small touches that lift morale.

See [[6 - Technical Rehearsals]] for the phase overview.

### Performance

- Coordinate opening-night and closing-night traditions.
- Capture moments from the run for the cast photo book.
- Be present in the lobby and backstage for key performances.

See [[7 - Performance]] for the phase overview.

### Post-Production

- Coordinate closing-night gifts, photo books, and farewell rituals.
- Document the production's "magical moments" for future reference.
- Reflect on what culture moments landed and what to adjust next time.

See [[8 - Post-Production]] for the phase overview.
