# Encore Script Notation

*Standard symbols used by Stage Managers when annotating scripts and prompt books. Companion reference to [[Stage Manager]] and [[0.0 - Show Bible|Show Bible]].*

## Stage Positions

- **USR:** Upstage Right
- **USC:** Upstage Center
- **USL:** Upstage Left
- **CSR / CSL / C:** Center Stage Right / Left / Center
- **DSR:** Downstage Right
- **DSC:** Downstage Center
- **DSL:** Downstage Left
- **SL / SR:** Stage Left / Stage Right

## Movement

- **X:** Cross to (move from one place to another)
- **→:** Moves to (or toward)
- **ENT:** Enters
- **EXT:** Exits

## Calling Cues

- **GO:** The universal term used when calling the show for a cue to happen.

## Cue Categories

The Encore default highlight-color convention:

| Type | Prefix | Example | Highlight color |
|------|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Lights | LX | LX 2-1 | Yellow |
| Sound | SD | SD 1-A | Blue |
| Music | M | M 1-A | Green |
| Props / Scenery | P (props), SC (scenery) | P 3, SC 2 | Pink |

A show may adopt a different convention if the [[Stage Manager]] documents it on the first page of the [[0.0 - Show Bible|Show Bible]]. Whatever colors you choose, stay consistent across the whole prompt book and tell the board operators which color means what.
