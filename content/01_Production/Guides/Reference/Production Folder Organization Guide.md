# Production Folder Organization Guide

## Overview
This guide covers the complete structure and implementation of the 01_Productions folder within Encore's Google Drive system. The Productions folder uses the most complex structure since it's our most involved work, requiring detailed organization for successful collaboration across all phases of production.

**Purpose:** Organize all documents, materials, and communications needed to produce a show from inception through archive.

## Folder Details
### 00_Producer
**Access:** Executive Only
**Purpose:** High-level production management and sensitive materials

#### Financial/
  - **Master_Budget_and_Payment_Tracking.xlsx** - Excel file with multiple tabs:

      - Master Budget tab

      - Payment Tracking tab

      - Department Budget breakdowns

      - Cash flow projections

      - Financial reports

#### Rights_and_Agreements/
  - Performance rights documentation

  - Licensing agreements

  - Legal correspondence with rights holders

#### Contracts/
  - **Creative_Team/** - Director, [[Music Supervisor Director|Music Director]], Choreographer contracts

  - **Design_Team/** - All designer contracts and agreements

  - **Production_Ops_and_Crews/** - Technical staff, crew, and operations contracts

#### Mission_Vision_and_EFI_Targets/
  - Production-specific mission statement

  - Educational goals and targets

  - Vision alignment documentation

### 01_Rehearsal
**Access:** All Teams
**Purpose:** Cast-accessible materials only

#### Cast_Information/
  - Contact information for cast members

  - Character assignments

  - Emergency contact information

  - **Note:** No headshots stored here

#### Daily_Calls/
  - Daily rehearsal call sheets

  - Schedule updates and changes

  - Attendance tracking

#### Script_Score_and_Tracks/
  - Master script and score

  - Music tracks and accompaniment files

  - Updated pages and revisions

#### Calendars/
  - Rehearsal schedule

  - Performance calendar

  - Important dates and deadlines

#### Contact_Lists/
  - Production team contact information

  - Emergency contacts

  - Key personnel directory

#### Rehearsal_Policies_and_Expectations/
  - Rehearsal room rules and expectations

  - Attendance policies

  - Safety guidelines

### 02_Designs
**Access:** Production Team
**Purpose:** Creative team workspace and design development

#### 00_Rehearsal_and_Production_Meeting_Reports/
  - **Rehearsal_Reports/** - Daily rehearsal documentation

  - **Production_Meeting_Reports/** - Production team meeting notes

#### 01_Director/
  - **Vision_Statement/** - Director's artistic vision and concept

  - **Character_Breakdowns/** - Character analysis and development

  - **Scene_Breakdowns/** - Scene-by-scene analysis

  - **Reference_Materials/** - Inspiration and research materials

#### 02_Sets/ through 07_Hair_and_Makeup/
Each design folder contains:

  - **Design_Package/** - Complete design deliverables per Pre-Production Guide

  - **Photos/** - Progress photos and documentation

  - **Audio_Files/** - Sound design only (instead of Photos)

#### 08_Tech_and_Strike/
  - **Tech_Reports/** - Technical rehearsal documentation

  - **Strike_and_Returns/** - Equipment return and strike coordination

  - **Storage/** - Storage location documentation

### 03_Auditions
**Access:** Mixed (Production Team + Public Links)

#### Audition_Material/ ← PUBLIC LINK
**Public Access via Link**

  - **Show_Overview_Calendar_and_Requirements.doc** - All audition info in one document

  - **Audition_Sides/** - Scene excerpts for auditions

  - **Audition_Songs/** - Music for auditions

#### Casting/ ← PRIVATE
**Production Team Only**

  - **Audition_Forms_and_Evaluations/** - Audition documentation

  - **Callback_Notes/** - Callback process documentation

  - **Final_Casting_Decisions/** - Casting choices and rationale

### 04_Performances
**Access:** Production Team + Crew, Ops, & FOH

#### Performance_Reports/
  - Nightly performance reports

  - Issue tracking and notes

  - Performance quality documentation

#### Front_of_House/
  - House management procedures

  - Patron services documentation

  - Lobby and concession coordination

#### Box_Office_Reports/
  - Ticket sales tracking

  - Attendance records

  - Revenue reports

### 05_Marketing
**Access:** Production Team + Crew, Ops, & FOH

#### Working Folders (All PRIVATE):
  - **Promotional_Materials/** - Posters, flyers, print materials

  - **Marketing_Campaigns/** - Social media calendars, email campaigns

  - **Social_Media_Content/** - Posts, stories, content creation

  - **Cast_Headshots/** - Professional cast photography

  - **Rehearsal_Footage/** - Behind-the-scenes video content

  - **Production_Footage/** - Performance and dress rehearsal video

  - **Program_Design/** - Program layout and sponsor information

  - **Shirts_and_Sizing/** - Merchandise and cast sizing information

  - **Press_and_Media/** - Press releases and media relations

#### Public_Marketing_Materials/ ← PUBLIC LINK
**Public Access via Link**

  - Approved promotional materials only

  - Community-accessible photos and videos

  - Press-ready materials

  - **Workflow:** Marketing team copies approved materials here from working folders

## Setup Process
### Phase 1: Folder Creation
1.  > **Create main production folder:** [Show_Name_Year]

2.  > **Build complete subfolder structure** following the template above

3.  > **Set initial permissions** according to access matrix

4.  > **Create public link folders** for Audition_Material and Public_Marketing_Materials

### Phase 2: Initial Population
1.  > **Upload core documents** to appropriate folders

2.  > **Set up Excel tracking file** in Financial folder

3.  > **Create initial calendars and contact lists**

4.  > **Establish public links** and test access

### Phase 3: Team Orientation
1.  > **Train Production Manager** on folder management

2.  > **Orient creative team** on design folder structure

3.  > **Educate cast** on rehearsal folder contents

4.  > **Establish file naming protocols**

### Phase 4: Ongoing Management
1.  > **Regular permission audits** as team changes

2.  > **Archive outdated materials** systematically

3.  > **Update public folders** as materials are approved

4.  > **Maintain folder organization** throughout production

## Best Practices
### File Naming Conventions
  - **Documents:** [TYPE]_[SHOW]_[DATE]_[VERSION]

  - **Photos:** [DATE]_[EVENT]_[DESCRIPTION]

  - **Videos:** [DATE]_[TYPE]_[SHOW]_[DESCRIPTION]

### Public Link Management
  - **Test links regularly** to ensure functionality

  - **Update public materials promptly** when internal versions change

  - **Remove outdated materials** after production closes

  - **Monitor content** to ensure only approved materials are public

### Archive Process
1.  > **Complete final documentation** in all folders

2.  > **Ensure all financial tracking** is up to date

3.  > **Move entire production folder** to Archived_Shows

4.  > **Remove cast and crew access** after archive

5.  > **Maintain Executive access** for historical reference

### Security Guidelines
  - **Regular permission reviews** especially during casting changes

  - **Prompt access removal** when team members leave

  - **Consistent use of Encore accounts** when possible

  - **Protection of student information** per privacy guidelines

## Troubleshooting
### Common Issues
**"I can't access a folder"**

  - Verify your role matches the permission matrix

  - Confirm you're using your Encore Google account

  - Contact Production Manager for access verification

**"Public link isn't working"**

  - Check that link hasn't been changed or expired

  - Verify you're accessing the correct subfolder

  - Contact Marketing team for current links

**"Folder is getting cluttered"**

  - Follow file naming conventions consistently

  - Archive old versions in dated subfolders

  - Use Google Drive's built-in version history

### Getting Help
  - **Access Issues:** Production Manager

  - **Technical Problems:** IT Support or Google Workspace admin

  - **Structure Questions:** Refer to this guide

  - **Training Needs:** Department Directors

## Conclusion
The Production folder structure supports Encore's collaborative approach to creating exceptional theater while maintaining the organization needed for complex projects. By following this guide, production teams can focus on creating great art rather than searching for information.

**Key Success Factors:**

  - **Consistent implementation** across all productions

  - **Regular maintenance** and permission management

  - **Clear communication** about folder purposes and access

  - **Ongoing training** for new team members

This structure grows with each production, incorporating lessons learned while maintaining the consistency that allows teams to work efficiently and effectively in service of our young artists and our artistic mission.
