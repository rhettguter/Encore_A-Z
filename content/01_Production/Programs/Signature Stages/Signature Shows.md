# Signature Shows

*Encore's flagship advanced productions. Part of [[0.0 - Signature Stages]] — full, story-driven musicals with complete production values, operating at the intersection of rigorous artistic training and compassionate mentorship.*

## Program Overview

Signature Shows operate at the intersection of rigorous artistic training and compassionate mentorship, demanding professional-level commitment while maintaining the educational support that allows young artists to take creative risks. Each full, story-driven musical features complete production values including sets, costumes, lighting, and sound, running for approximately a dozen performances.

Our staff- and faculty-led mentorship creates a safe space for young artists to discover, develop, and grow their unique voice while immersing themselves in storytelling and the art of performance.

## Key Features

- **Professional-level artistic training** with educational support.
- **Full story-driven musicals** with complete production values (sets, costumes, lighting, sound).
- **Approximately 12 performances** per production.
- **Staff and faculty-led mentorship** throughout the process.
- **Safe space for creative risk-taking** and artistic growth.
- **Advanced character development** and storytelling immersion.
- **Industry-standard rehearsal practices** and expectations.

The mentorship creates a safe space for young artists to develop and refine their unique voice while gaining advanced confidence in their performance abilities and collaborative leadership skills.

## Acting Theory Curriculum

Signature Shows commonly pair the production process with a weekly acting class grounded in major theorists of stage acting — Linklater, Chekhov, Bogart, Stanislavski, Hagen, Meisner, Johnstone — applied to the production's actual script. The curriculum is one of the things that makes Signature Shows distinct from [[Emerging Artist Shows]]: artists are now thinking critically about *why* and *how* acting works, not just executing.

## Where Signature Shows Fit

Signature Shows are the **entry point into [[0.0 - Signature Stages]]** — the natural next step after a young artist has progressed through [[0.0 - Junior Stages]] ([[Camps]] → [[Junior Shows]] → [[Emerging Artist Shows]]). The next step beyond Signature Shows is [[Show with a Pro]], which adds integrated professional guest artists to the Signature Show format.

## Operational Notes

A Signature Show is a full Encore production process — see [[0.0 - Production Bible|Production Bible]] for the framework. The [[Director's Guide]] applies in full, especially the sections on working with young artists and the canonical Transitions section. [[Music Supervisor's Guide]] and [[Choreographer's Guide]] cover the music and movement disciplines. The full role pool ([[Director]], [[Music Supervisor Director]], [[Choreographer]], [[Stage Manager]], [[Production Manager]], [[Technical Director]], all designers) is typically engaged.
