# Production Programs — README

*Home for Encore's two production ladders — Junior Stages and Signature Stages — the named program families that mount full theatrical productions. The umbrella overview lives at [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]] in [[02_Education/Programs/00_README|Education Programs]].*

## What lives here

**Junior Stages/** — the foundation production ladder:

- [[0.0 - Junior Stages]] — tier overview
- [[Camps]] — ages 6–9, week-long intensive
- [[Junior Shows]] — first foundational full-length productions (30–45 min)
- [[Emerging Artist Shows]] — next-step productions (60–90 min)

**Signature Stages/** — the advanced production ladder:

- [[0.0 - Signature Stages]] — tier overview
- [[Signature Shows]] — flagship advanced productions (~12 performances)
- [[Show with a Pro]] — Signature productions with integrated professional guest artists

## What doesn't live here

- The umbrella narrative and pathway story lives at [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]] in [[02_Education/Programs/00_README|Education Programs]].
- Education-only program offerings (year-round ensembles, accessibility programming, leadership programs) live in [[02_Education/Programs/00_README|02_Education/Programs/]] — [[Aspire]], [[Page to Stage]], [[Sensory-Friendly Performances]], [[Youth in the Booth]], [[Youth Leadership Program]].
- Specific production folders (e.g., "Lion King Kids 2026") live in Google Drive, not in this vault.
