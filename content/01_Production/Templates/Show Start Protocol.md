# Show Start Protocol

Show Pre-start checklist protocol- SHOW DOES NOT START WITHOUT THE VERBAL **GO** FROM THE **[[Stage Manager|STAGE MANAGER]] ONLY**

Pre House Open

  - Ensure all cast members are present and accounted for-using the check in sheet.

  - Mic Check all Cast Members

  - Ensure all presets and set pieces are placed before the house opens

  - Double check spike tape on stage and ensure cords taped down

  - Make sure blue lights are on

  - Ensure all cast members off stage and out of the house

  - Give the house to the house manager

Once house is open

  - Give 30, 15, 10, and 5 minute reminders to places to cast members

  - Ensure all cast members are in the dressing room ready for places at least 5 minutes before places

  - SM verbal confirmation prior to show start from:

      - Lights Operator- in position and light board is ready to run the show

      - Sound Operator- in position and sound board is ready to run the show

      - Backstage Manager- in position and all cast members are mic'd and in costume ready to start

      - House Manager- Lobby is clear and patrons are in their seats- House manager gives the house back to the SM.

Intermission Checklist

  - Make sure that Persephone's Suitcase is out of the platform

  - Eurydice & Persephone Quick Changes set

  - Hermes Messenger and Emma have switched mics

  - Blocks are in the right spot

  - Give 5 min to places, and places call. Black out is cue for Music to start
