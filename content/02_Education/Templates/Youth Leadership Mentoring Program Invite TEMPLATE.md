## Opportunity Overview
Have you ever wanted to work with young artists in a leadership role? Now is your chance! We are looking for youth directors, music directors, choreographers, stage managers, and backstage coordinators for our upcoming camps.

This unique program is designed to **help prepare you to take on leadership positions** in the theater industry. Encore provides our youth leaders with amazing professional adult mentors to help them learn the skills necessary to teach and guide the young participants in our camps.

Youth leaders trained by Encore will teach and inspire kids to have an exceptional camp experience while also enhancing their own skills in this art form.

## The Experience
Under the supervision and direction of Encore adult staff, we are excited to provide not only a fun week of singing, dancing, and performing for the children, but also a meaningful leadership opportunity for you.

**What You'll Gain:**

  - Professional mentorship from experienced theater educators

  - Real-world leadership experience with young artists

  - Skills in teaching, directing, and production management

  - Portfolio-building experience for college applications or career development

  - Community service hours *(if applicable)*

  - Letter of recommendation from Encore staff
