# Emerging Artist Shows

*Encore's next-step productions for young artists ready to take on greater challenges. The advanced tier of [[0.0 - Junior Stages]].*

## Program Overview

These 60–90 minute productions provide a pathway for young artists to embrace greater challenges while developing more sophisticated performance skills. Students begin to explore deeper character work, more demanding vocal techniques, and collaborative ensemble skills.

Emerging Artist Shows feature enhanced production values including more detailed costumes, sophisticated lighting, and fuller orchestration — while students also begin to take on backstage responsibilities, learning about the technical aspects of theater production and developing leadership skills.

## Key Features

- **60–90 minute full-length productions** — meaningful theater at a sustainable pace for developing artists.
- **Enhanced production values** with detailed costumes and sophisticated lighting.
- **Deeper character work** and more advanced vocal techniques.
- **Collaborative ensemble skill development.** The cast becomes a real team.
- **Introduction to backstage responsibilities.** Young artists start seeing the show from multiple angles.
- **Leadership skill development opportunities.** Older Emerging Artists begin to mentor newer ones.
- **Fuller orchestration and sophisticated staging.**

## Where Emerging Artist Shows Fit

Emerging Artist Shows are the **top tier of [[0.0 - Junior Stages]]** — after [[Camps]] and [[Junior Shows]], and before young artists move into [[0.0 - Signature Stages]]. This is where the educational pathway transitions from "introduction to theater" to "I'm a serious young theater artist."

## Operational Notes

The [[Director]] is now able to do real character work — see [[Director's Guide]]. The [[Music Supervisor Director|Music Director]] introduces healthy belt, harmony parts, and more demanding rep — see [[Music Supervisor's Guide]] for vocal pedagogy. The [[Choreographer]] can layer complexity for mixed-ability casts — see [[Choreographer's Guide]]. Backstage responsibilities give the [[Stage Manager]] and the design team opportunities to mentor [[Youth in the Booth]] participants.
