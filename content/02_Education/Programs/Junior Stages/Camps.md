# Camps

*Encore's week-long intensive format for our youngest artists (ages 6–9). The first tier of [[0.0 - Junior Stages]]. Presented by the [[Youth Leadership Program]].*

## Program Overview

The Camps leverage learning opportunities for our youngest artists (ages 6–9) AND our most experienced young adults (the [[Youth Leadership Program]] participants who act as camp counselors). Inspired by theater camps, shows rehearse over about a week, with performances running about 30 minutes.

The format is charming, endearing, and creates a fun, supportive environment — often a young person's first introduction to Encore.

## Key Features

- **Week-long intensive format.** Rehearse and perform within a single week.
- **[[Youth Leadership Program]] mentors serve as counselors.** This is the program's secret sauce: the older young artists develop teaching and leadership skills while supporting the younger ones.
- **Age-appropriate material and expectations.** Material and process designed specifically for ages 6–9.
- **Focus on fun and foundational skills.** Joy and curiosity come first; just enough structure to model what real theater feels like.
- **Typical run:** 2–3 performances.

## Where Camps Fit

Camps are the **entry point** of [[0.0 - Junior Stages]] and Encore's pathway as a whole. Many young artists experience their first Encore moment here. From Camps, the natural next step is [[Junior Shows]], then [[Emerging Artist Shows]], then [[Signature Shows]].

## Operational Notes

The success of Camps depends heavily on the [[Youth Leadership Program]] counselor structure. See [[Youth Leadership Program]] for how older young artists are prepared for and deployed in this role. Also see [[Rehearsal Culture]] and [[Ten Agreements]] for the expectations all counselors are expected to model.
