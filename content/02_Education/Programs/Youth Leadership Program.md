# Youth Leadership Program

*Encore's leadership-development pathway. Young performers ages 14–18 step up to mentor fellow young artists while being mentored by experienced theater staff.*

## Program Overview

Young performers ages 14–18 step up to mentor fellow young artists while being mentored by experienced theater staff. Participants choose a specialized theater area for deep learning, collaborate on show design and staging, develop teaching and mentorship skills, and practice leadership in real production environments.

**Program elements**

- Choose specialized theater area for deep learning
- Collaborate on show design, structure, and staging
- Develop teaching and mentorship skills
- Learn creative design and production coordination
- Practice leadership in real production environments

**Skills developed**

- Leadership and responsibility
- Teaching and communication
- Creative problem-solving
- Production management
- Peer mentorship

## Where Youth Leadership Plugs In

YLP participants commonly serve as:

- **Camp counselors** for the [[Camps]] program — the model explicitly relies on this.
- **Cast leaders or peer mentors** within [[Signature Shows]] and [[Show with a Pro]] productions.
- **Assistant roles** in production: see [[Assistant Stage Manager]] and [[Production Assistant]] for the formal production-side leadership pathway.

## Where This Fits in the Education Programs

Youth Leadership Program runs alongside [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]] rather than within it. Many YLP participants are simultaneously cast members in Junior or Signature productions.

The sister program for technical theater is [[Youth in the Booth]].
