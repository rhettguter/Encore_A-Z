# Youth in the Booth

*Encore's technical theater leadership pathway. Teens ages 14–18 gain hands-on experience with theatrical technical production under professional guidance.*

## Program Overview

Teens ages 14–18 gain hands-on experience with theatrical technical production under professional guidance — exploring real technical theater careers while contributing meaningfully to Encore productions.

**Technical areas**

- Lighting design and operation
- Sound engineering and design
- [[Stage Manager|Stage management]] and coordination
- Production planning and execution

**Career pathway focus**

- Explore technical theater careers
- Develop professional technical skills
- Understand collaborative processes
- Experience in leadership and critical thinking

## How It Connects to Productions

Youth in the Booth participants work as board operators, ASM/PA contributors, and technical crew on Encore productions. They are supervised by the [[Technical Director]] and partner closely with the [[Stage Manager]] during tech and run. See [[PM and TD Collaboration]] for how the supervising trio (PM, TD, SM) works together.

## Where This Fits in the Education Programs

Youth in the Booth runs alongside the production ladder rather than within it. The sister program for performance/creative leadership is [[Youth Leadership Program]].
