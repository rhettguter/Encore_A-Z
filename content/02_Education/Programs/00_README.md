# Education Programs — README

*Documentation for Encore's education-track program offerings — the programs that run alongside the production ladders rather than mounting full productions. The umbrella overview of all programs (including Junior and Signature Stages) lives here at [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]].*

## What lives here

**[[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]]** — the umbrella overview of the full production pathway and how all Encore programs fit together.

**Alongside the production ladder** — programs that complement Junior and Signature Stages without being part of a casting-driven production:

- [[Aspire]] — Aspire Performing Co. (year-round show choir)
- [[Page to Stage]] — three-part theater education for visiting groups
- [[Sensory-Friendly Performances]] — accessibility programming
- [[Youth in the Booth]] — ages 14–18 technical theater leadership
- [[Youth Leadership Program]] — ages 14–18 creative and performance leadership

## What doesn't live here

- **Production ladders.** Junior Stages ([[Camps]], [[Junior Shows]], [[Emerging Artist Shows]]) and Signature Stages ([[Signature Shows]], [[Show with a Pro]]) live in [[01_Production/Programs/00_README|01_Production/Programs/]].
- The Education department's general curriculum frameworks (rehearsal culture, audition prep, table work, ensemble games) live in `../Guides/`.
- Role definitions for educators live in `../Roles/`.
- Specific production folders (e.g., "Lion King Kids 2026") live in Google Drive, not in this vault.
