# Signature Stages

*The heart of Encore's educational mission. The advanced tier of [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]] — serving students who are ready for professional-level artistic challenges within a supportive educational framework.*

## Overview

Where [[0.0 - Junior Stages]] introduces young artists to theater and builds their foundation, the Signature Stages take that foundation to professional-level rigor. Students at this tier are not just performing — they are training as serious young artists.

Two program formats live in the Signature Stages:

### [[Signature Shows]] — Ages 12–18

Full, story-driven musicals with complete production values, running approximately a dozen performances. Operate at the intersection of rigorous artistic training and compassionate mentorship — demanding professional-level commitment while maintaining educational support that allows young artists to take creative risks.

Staff- and faculty-led mentorship creates a safe space for young artists to discover, develop, and grow their unique voice while immersing themselves in storytelling and the art of performance.

### [[Show with a Pro]]

Signature Shows that integrate professional guest artists — designers, performers, music directors — throughout the entire process. The bridge between educational theater and professional practice. Guests conduct specialized workshops, model professional rehearsal practices, and work alongside students as cast members.

## Why "Signature"

These are the productions that define Encore publicly. A Signature Show is the kind of show people in the community plan their year around. They demand the most from young artists and they deliver the most in return — both as performances and as educational experiences.

## Operational Notes

A Signature Show is a full Encore production process. See [[0.0 - Production Bible|Production Bible]] for the framework. The [[Director's Guide]] applies in full; [[Choreographer's Guide]] and [[Music Supervisor's Guide]] for those roles. [[Show with a Pro]] adds the layer of integrating professional guests — see that doc for the additional considerations.
