# Encore at a Glance

## Your First Steps to Getting Involved

## Who We Are

Encore Performing Arts inspires and empowers young artists to discover and develop important life skills through unparalleled arts education, mentorship, and extraordinary theater experiences.

Simply put: We teach life skills through stage skills.

*Full mission, vision, and values: [[This is Encore]].*

## Where We Are

- **Location:** St. George, Utah
- **Communities Served:** Southern Utah including St. George, Hurricane, Washington, Ivins, and surrounding areas
- **Facilities:** The Historic Electric Theater — Downtown St. George with classrooms upstairs and downstairs plus full theater venue

## Our Impact

- **Young Artists Served Annually:** 300+
- **Years in Operation:** 4+ years (established 2020)
- **Productions Per Year:** 6-8 major productions plus showcases
- **Educational Programs:** 15+ different classes and program offerings

## How to Get Involved

### For Young Artists

**Ages 8-18 Welcome!**

- **Start Here:** Attend one of our camps or workshops.
- **Next Steps:** Audition for age-appropriate productions.
- **Get Info:** Visit encorepa.org or call (435) 414-0049.
- **Follow Us:** @encorestg (Facebook & Instagram)

### For Families

- **Learn More:** Attend a production to see our work.
- **Stay Connected:** Join our email list for audition announcements.
- **Support:** Consider volunteer opportunities or donations.
- **Questions?** Contact us at hello@encorepa.org or (435) 414-0049.

### For Volunteers

**We Need You!**

- **Production Support:** Backstage crew, costume help, set building
- **Events:** Cast parties, community events, fundraisers
- **Administrative:** Registration, communications, office support
- **Special Skills:** Photography, marketing, technical expertise

Ready to help? Contact our Events Coordinator through hello@encorepa.org.

### For Donors & Sponsors

**Your Investment Changes Lives**

- **Individual Donations:** Support scholarships and programming.
- **Business Sponsorships:** Partner with us for community visibility.
- **Equipment Donations:** Help us maintain professional standards.
- **Legacy Giving:** Create lasting impact for future generations.

Learn more by contacting our [[Director of Development]] at fundraising@encorepa.org.

### For Professionals

**Share Your Expertise**

- **Guest Artists:** Work with our young artists in *Show with a Pro* productions.
- **Workshops:** Lead masterclasses and skill-building sessions.
- **Mentorship:** Guide our Youth Leadership Program participants.
- **Board Service:** Provide governance and strategic guidance.

Get involved by contacting hello@encorepa.org.

## Quick Facts

### Our Programs Include

- Emerging Artist Series Shows (ages 6-12)
- Signature Series Shows (ages 12-18)
- Aspire Show Choir (audition-based, ages 7-18)
- Shine Children's Choir
- Youth Leadership Program
- Summer/Holiday Camps
- Classes and Workshops
- Page to Stage educational field trips

### What Makes Us Special

- Professional-quality productions with educational focus
- Peer mentorship through Youth Leadership Program
- Phone-free learning environments
- Emphasis on emotional intelligence and life skills
- Inclusive community welcoming all experience levels
- Sensory-friendly performances available

### Our Annual Highlights

- *A Christmas Carol: The New Musical* (Holiday tradition)
- *Aspire's Villains Night* (Fall showcase)
- *Aspire's Bright Lights of Broadway* (Spring showcase)
- Full-scale productions of major musicals throughout the year
