# Encoreverse — Home

*Welcome. This vault is the canonical knowledge base for Encore Performing Arts. Use the entry points below to find what you need; the full index follows.*

## Start Here

**New to Encore?** Read [[Encore at a Glance]] first, then [[This is Encore]] for the mission, vision, and values. Live by the [[Ten Agreements]].

**New to the vault?** Read [[Encore at a Glance]] for the org snapshot, then browse the index below.

## I'm trying to…

**Direct a show.** Start with [[Director]] (role doc with phase-by-phase responsibilities) and [[Director's Guide]] (Encore-specific philosophy — Heart Over Art, working with young artists, transitions). Use the [[Director Checklist]] as your tick-box working tool. Reference [[0.0 - Production Bible|Production Bible]] for the full framework.

**Choreograph a show.** Start with [[Choreographer]] and [[Choreographer's Guide]]. Use the [[Choreographer Checklist]].

**Music-direct a show.** Start with [[Music Supervisor Director]] and [[Music Supervisor's Guide]].

**Stage-manage a show.** Start with [[Stage Manager]] and the [[0.0 - Show Bible|Show Bible]] guide. Reference [[Encore Script Notation]] for cue symbols.

**Produce a show.** Start with [[Producer]] and the [[0.0 - Production Bible|Production Bible]].

**Run production operations.** [[Production Manager]] is the day-to-day project manager. [[Technical Director]] handles technical execution. See [[PM and TD Collaboration]] for how the PM/TD/SM trio divides work.

**Design a show.** Find your role: [[01_Production/Roles/Design Team/Set Designer|Set Designer]] · [[Costume Designer]] · [[Light Designer]] · [[Sound Designer]] · [[Prop Designer]].

**Volunteer.** Read [[Volunteer Handbook]] and the [[Ten Agreements]]. See [[Volunteer Coordinator]] for who to contact and [[Volunteer Integration]] for where volunteers fit.

**Teach a class or workshop.** [[Director of Education]] is your home base. See the Education Guides: [[Rehearsal Culture]], [[Audition Prep]], [[Activities, Games, and Endings]].

**Run a production program.** [[0.0 - The Stages of Encore]] is the umbrella. Junior Stages (production ladder): [[Camps]] → [[Junior Shows]] → [[Emerging Artist Shows]]. Signature Stages (advanced ladder): [[Signature Shows]] → [[Show with a Pro]].

**Run an education program.** Programs that run alongside the production ladder: [[Aspire]] (show choir), [[Youth Leadership Program]], [[Youth in the Booth]], [[Sensory-Friendly Performances]], [[Page to Stage]].

**Cast a show.** See [[3 - Casting]] for the full process and [[Audition Form]] for the standard form.

**Get through tech week.** [[Tech Week Essentials]] is the survival guide. [[6 - Technical Rehearsals]] is the phase walkthrough.

**Onboard a new team member.** [[Onboarding and Team Hiring]] is the process; [[Encore at a Glance]] is the orientation.

**Run Launch Day (parent meeting).** See [[Launch Day]] for the community welcome event and [[Parent Meeting Outline]] for the parent-meeting portion's agenda.

**Understand the org structure.** [[Departments and Roles]] is the org chart. [[Production Hierarchy]] explains the within-a-show team structure.

**Find financial information.** [[Budget Management]], [[Payment Structure]], [[Finance Director]].

**Govern Encore.** [[Board of Directors]], [[Annual Board Meeting Schedule]], [[Annual Planning Cycles]], [[EFI Targets Framework]].

**Work on the tech stack.** [[Tech Stack Overview]] is the inventory. [[Monthly Bookkeeping Workflow]] is the recurring rhythm. [[Mentor Portal]] is the custom team portal — see [[Portal Architecture]] and [[Portal Pipeline]] for how content flows from vault to portal.
