# Production Historian

## Position Overview

The Production Historian is a parent volunteer role responsible for capturing and preserving the memories of the production. Through photography, video, and archival organization, the Production Historian creates a lasting record of rehearsals, performances, and behind-the-scenes moments for Encore's community. All media captured in this role is shared with the production team for distribution and archive — never published independently.

**Reports to:** [[Production Manager]] **Collaborates closely with:** [[Stage Manager]], Marketing/Social Media lead

## Key Responsibilities

### Photography & Videography

- Take high-quality photographs of rehearsals, performances, and behind-the-scenes moments.
- Record video clips of significant events, rehearsals, and shows, capturing both key scenes and candid moments.

### Organizing Media

- Catalog and organize all photos and videos taken throughout the production.
- Create clearly labeled folders for easy access and retrieval, and share the organized media with the [[Production Manager]].

### Creating an Archive

- Compile photos, programs, and other memorabilia into a physical scrapbook or digital archive and share with the Production Manager.

### Social Media & Publicity

- Share all content with the designated Marketing/Social Media contact for use on social media and public platforms.
- Never share photos or videos on your personal social media accounts.
- Communicate with the Marketing/Social Media lead when media is ready for publication.

### Coordinating with Other Volunteers

- Work closely with other parent volunteers — costumes, props, refreshments — to document their contributions.
- Ensure a comprehensive record of all who participate in the production.

### Distributing Memories

- Ensure the [[Production Manager]] and [[Stage Manager]] have digital access to all photos and videos for distribution to the appropriate people.

### Event Documentation

- Document special events related to the production, including cast parties, fundraising events, and community outreach activities.
- Capture both candid and posed shots that reflect the spirit and camaraderie of the cast and crew.

## Additional Guidelines

- **Permission & Privacy:** Always confirm with the [[Stage Manager]] or [[Production Manager]] that all cast members have completed the media release portion of their contract before sharing any photos or video publicly. Parents give permission when signing the Encore contract — but verify before publishing.
- **Backup Copies:** Maintain backup copies of all media files to prevent loss due to technical issues.
- **Creativity:** Use varied formats and styles to make the archive engaging and memorable.
- **Timeliness:** Stay on top of documentation throughout the production to avoid a backlog. Regularly update the archive and submit content to the social media lead.

## Working Conditions

- Presence required at designated rehearsals, performances, and special events.
- Volunteer role — time commitment varies by production phase, increasing during tech and run.
- Must follow all Encore policies regarding media, privacy, and conduct while working with young performers.

## Success Measures

- A complete, well-organized archive of media delivered to the Production Manager by the end of the run.
- Social media content submitted in a timely manner throughout the production.
- All privacy and permission guidelines followed without exception.
- Positive documentation of the production that reflects Encore's culture and community.
