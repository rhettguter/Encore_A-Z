# Volunteer Integration

## Working Draft - Refined Version

## Volunteers: The Backbone of Encore's Success

Volunteers are not just helpers at Encore — they are integral members of our organizational family who make our mission possible. Our volunteer network amplifies every department's capacity, extends our reach into the community, and brings diverse talents and perspectives that enhance everything we do.

This document shows how volunteers integrate seamlessly into our organizational structure, supporting both Foundation coordination functions and Mission Department delivery, while being guided by our Executive Leadership's vision of inclusive, community-centered arts education.

## Volunteer Integration by Department

Volunteers work alongside paid staff in every area of our organization through both **Ongoing Organizational Roles** (regular positions with consistent responsibilities) and **Event-Based Support Roles** (flexible opportunities for specific events or needs). This structure allows community members to engage at the level that fits their availability while ensuring consistent support for our programs.

### Culture Department

*Volunteers enhance our welcoming atmosphere and community connections.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Volunteers extend Culture's reach by multiplying our capacity to create positive first impressions and meaningful experiences. They embody Encore's values in every interaction, turning every patron touchpoint into a community-building opportunity.

#### Ongoing Organizational Roles

- **Events Coordinator:** Plan, coordinate, and execute culture-building events (Volunteer/Paid Position).
- **Cast Party Coordinator:** Plan and prepare memorable cast parties for each show.
- **Cast Party Coordinator Assistant:** Assist in planning and preparing cast parties.

#### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Ushers:** Greet patrons and provide hospitality during performances.
- **Box Office Support:** Assist with ticket sales during high-volume periods.
- **Event Setup/Cleanup Crew:** Handle logistics and setup for special events.
- **Event Volunteers:** Support events and special occasions.
- **Registration Assistants:** Assist with check-in processes at events.
- **Hospitality Team:** Provide refreshments and guest services.
- **Sensory-Friendly Assistants:** Support families during accessible performances.

**Impact:** Volunteers enable Culture Department to touch every person who enters Encore's world, creating the positive experiences that build lifelong supporters and advocates.

### Marketing, Branding and Messaging Department

*Volunteers amplify our voice and extend our community reach.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Volunteers serve as authentic community ambassadors, sharing Encore's story through personal networks and documenting our impact in ways that resonate with diverse audiences.

#### Ongoing Organizational Roles

- **Social Media / Historian:** Capture and manage photos/videos, oversee blog content (Volunteer/Paid Position).
- **Promotion Coordinator:** Manage permits, yard signs, and promotional materials.
- **Printing Coordinator:** Coordinate all organizational printing needs.
- **Street Banners Permits Coordinator:** Handle permit processes and banner coordination.
- **T-Shirt Coordinator:** Manage merchandise and apparel for events and productions.

#### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Community Photographers/Videographers:** Document specific events and productions.
- **Outreach Ambassadors:** Represent Encore at community events and festivals.
- **Administrative Support:** Assist with filing, data entry, and mailings.

**Impact:** Volunteers provide credible, grassroots marketing that money can't buy, while extending MBM's capacity to document and share our story across the community.

### Production

*Volunteers bring productions to life through hands-on artistry.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Volunteers work side-by-side with professional staff to create the magic of live theater, contributing skilled labor, creative energy, and community investment that makes ambitious productions possible.

#### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Backstage Crew:** Assist with costume changes and scene transitions during performances.
- **Load-In/Strike Teams:** Help with set construction and removal for specific productions.
- **Costume Workshop:** Assist with fittings, repairs, and costume organization.
- **Props Teams:** Create, maintain, and manage props for specific productions.

**Impact:** Volunteers make it possible to produce high-quality theater that would otherwise be beyond our budget, while bringing community investment directly into our artistic process.

### Education

*Volunteers extend our educational reach and mentorship capacity.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Volunteers multiply our adult-to-student ratios, provide additional mentorship, and enable off-site learning experiences that broaden our educational impact.

#### Ongoing Organizational Roles

- **Registration/Check-In Coordinator:** Manage registration processes for programs (Volunteer/Paid Position).
- **Friends of Theater / Sensory Performance Coordinator:** Coordinate accessibility programs and special needs outreach.

#### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Class & Workshop Assistants:** Support teachers during specific educational programs and camps.
- **Educational Chaperones:** Supervise field trips and off-site learning experiences.
- **Transportation Support:** Provide rides for students to events or field trips.

**Impact:** Volunteers enable smaller student-to-adult ratios, expanded programming, and personalized attention that transforms educational experiences from good to exceptional.

### Development

*Volunteers extend our fundraising reach through authentic community connections.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Volunteers leverage personal relationships and community networks to authentically share Encore's impact, making fundraising feel like community investment rather than institutional asking.

#### Ongoing Organizational Roles

- **Fundraising Assistant:** Provide ongoing administrative and operational support.
- **Individual Gifts & Sponsorship Development Specialist:** Cultivate donors and secure business sponsorships (Volunteer/Paid Position).

#### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Fundraising Event Teams:** Handle logistics and coordination for galas and special events.
- **Administrative Support:** Assist with database tasks and donor communication preparation.

**Impact:** Volunteers provide authentic testimonials, expand our donor network through personal relationships, and reduce event costs while increasing community investment.

## Specialized Accessibility Integration

*Volunteers extend our inclusive mission through dedicated accessibility support.*

**How Volunteers Integrate:** Specialized volunteers bring personal understanding and professional skills that make Encore more accessible, often contributing expertise that we couldn't afford to hire.

### Ongoing Organizational Roles

- **ASL Interpreters:** Provide regular interpretation services for programs and performances.

### Event-Based Support Roles

- **Sensory-Friendly Performance Assistants:** Provide specialized support during accessible performances.
- **Accessibility Event Support:** Assist with accommodations at specific events and programs.

**Impact:** Volunteers enable us to serve community members who might otherwise be excluded, turning accessibility from a goal into a lived reality.

## Volunteer Coordination Structure

- **Central Coordination:** Our [[05_Culture/Roles/Volunteer Coordinator]] ensures consistent recruitment, screening, and orientation while maintaining organizational standards and volunteer satisfaction.
- **Departmental Integration:** Each department head provides position-specific training and supervision, ensuring volunteers understand their role in achieving departmental goals.
- **Leadership Oversight:** Executive Leadership ensures volunteer integration supports our mission while maintaining the inclusive, welcoming culture that attracts community investment.

## The Volunteer Impact

### For Encore

- **Expanded Capacity:** Volunteers multiply our human resources exponentially.
- **Community Investment:** Volunteers become stakeholders with personal investment in our success.
- **Diverse Perspectives:** Community members bring fresh ideas and varied expertise.
- **Financial Sustainability:** Volunteer contributions enable programming that budget constraints might otherwise prevent.

### For Volunteers

- **Meaningful Contribution:** Direct involvement in young artists' growth and development.
- **Skill Development:** Opportunities to learn theater, event planning, and organizational skills.
- **Community Connection:** Relationships with like-minded community members and families.
- **Arts Access:** Behind-the-scenes involvement in professional-quality theater.

### For Young Artists

- **Adult Mentorship:** Additional caring adults invested in their success.
- **Community Connection:** Understanding that the broader community supports their artistic growth.
- **Real-World Skills:** Learning to work alongside community professionals and skilled volunteers.
- **Expanded Opportunities:** Access to programs and experiences that volunteer support makes possible.

## Conclusion

Volunteers are not supplemental to Encore's work — they are integral to our identity as a community-centered organization. Every volunteer hour contributed represents community investment in our young artists' futures. Every volunteer relationship built extends our network of advocates and supporters.

Through thoughtful integration of volunteers across all departments, Encore creates a model where professional excellence combines with grassroots community engagement, ensuring that our educational mission reaches every corner of our community while remaining financially sustainable.

Our volunteers don't just help us succeed — they help us define what success looks like: a thriving, inclusive, community-owned arts education organization where every young artist has the support they need to flourish.

Volunteers transform Encore from an organization that serves the community into an organization that IS the community.
